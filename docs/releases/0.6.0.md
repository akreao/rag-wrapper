## New
- Our own client patcher, offered on the How to connect page as "the new patcher" beside rpatchur, which stays the main download until the new one is confirmed on a player's Windows PC. It applies the same patches, shows the newest news post in a plain window, and updates itself from the portal. It keeps rpatchur's place in the patch list, so players can switch either way without downloading everything again (#52).
- A News screen in the desktop app edits the portal's news: one card per post, newest on top, and a new post starts with today's date. Saved posts show on the News page and in the patcher straight away, without a restart (#54).
- The Mods screen lists rAthena's own custom NPCs, such as the warper, healer, job master, quests and events, each with a description and a checkbox. They're all off until you switch them on, and load on the next restart of the game servers (#53).

## Server
- rAthena moved from b6f89ab to 3cf9899. Airships now stop inside the Juno, Einbroch and Lighthalzen airports. Players no longer get stuck on the old docks, which the current client's maps don't have. The Rachel Sanctuary exit and two rooms on the international airship are fixed too. The app offers it as a server update; your database is backed up first (#55).

## Before you update
- After updating the app, click **Update server** on the bar, then **Restart on the new version** when players can be disconnected. Until then the server keeps the airship bug.
- Players don't need to download anything. Anyone who wants to try the new patcher can download it from the How to connect page into the client folder.
